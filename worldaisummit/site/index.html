about

Where India's AI decisions meet deployment

India's next chapter of AI isn't being written in a lab. It's being built inside ration systems and bank cores, in state data centres and GCC floors, on UPI rails and in 22 languages, for the moment a few hundred million people show up at once.

World AI Summit 2026 brings together the people who decide how that happens: Secretaries and Mission Directors, regulators, CIOs, CTOs and heads of AI and data at India's banks, insurers, retailers and manufacturers, the leaders of Global Capability Centres, and the founders and investors building for them.

From compute costs and IndiaAI access to RBI and SEBI expectations, Bhashini and DPI, model risk, talent and the pilot-to-production gap, this is where they compare what is actually working.

No AI futurism. No vendor pitches. No slideware.

Just the deployments, the decisions and the numbers behind them.

Policy gets written in Delhi. It gets deployed in this room.

Theme 2026: AI for All: Accelerating India's Journey Towards Inclusive, Responsible and Future-Ready AI.

agenda

Seven tracks. One question each.

Every track is built around a decision someone in the room has to make this year. Sessions and speakers are being announced now.

01

Frontier Models & Compute

What does it cost to train and serve models in India, and who pays for the GPUs?

02

Sovereign AI & Geopolitics

How much of the AI stack does India need to own, and what happens when export rules change?

03

Enterprise AI in Production

Why do most pilots stall, and what did the ones now in production do differently?

04

Global Capability Centres (GCCs)

How does a GCC become the place the parent company's AI actually gets built?

05

Robotics, Agents & Embodied AI

Where is it safe to let agents and robots act on their own today, and who is accountable when they don't?

06

AI for Bharat

How do you build AI that works in 22 languages, on low-cost phones and patchy networks, in Tier-2 towns?

07

Capital, Founders & Exits

Who is funding Indian AI companies now, and what does an exit actually look like?

faq

Frequently asked questions

Is this a startup or developer conference?

No. There are no hackathons, no pitch stages and no coding workshops. World AI Summit is for the people who decide where AI gets deployed: secretaries and mission directors, regulators, CIOs, CTOs, CDOs, heads of AI and data, GCC leaders, and the founders and investors who build for them.

Is this a government event?

No. World AI Summit is organised independently by Elets Technomedia. Government is in the room as speakers and delegates, which is the point: it is the one room where policymakers, regulators and enterprise leaders sit together. In 2025 the Karnataka IT/BT Department was Host Partner and the IndiaAI Mission was Co-Host.

I lead data, risk or product, not IT. Is this for me?

Yes. Confirmed 2026 speakers include heads of data strategy, analytics, risk, digital banking and cybersecurity, not only CIOs. If you own an AI budget, a policy or a production system, this is your room. The name says World. The agenda says India.

Who qualifies for a pass?

Senior decision makers: Secretaries, Mission Directors and regulators; CIOs, CTOs, CDOs and CISOs; heads of AI, data and analytics; GCC leaders; founders and investors. Every application is reviewed. Seats are capped to keep the room senior. If your application fits a different pass, our team will contact you.

Can I transfer my pass to a colleague?

No. Passes are issued to a named delegate and cannot be shared or transferred.

Can I register for a single session?

No. Passes cover the summit, not individual sessions.

Do you offer group bookings?

Yes. Groups of 3 or more delegates from the same organisation get 10% off. Write to registration@worldaisummit.com with the names and designations.

How do I become a partner or sponsor?

Write to partnerships@worldaisummit.com for partnership, sponsorship and exhibition options, or see the partner page. For speaking and collaboration, write to secretariat@worldaisummit.com.

What are the World AI Awards?

The World AI Awards recognise AI that is actually working, across business innovation, public sector transformation, startups, leadership and platforms. Nominations are open on the awards page.

Where and when is the summit?

14-15 October 2026 at the Sheraton Grand Bangalore Hotel at Brigade Gateway, Bengaluru. It is the 2nd edition; the first was held at the same venue on 25-26 September 2025.
