# World AI Summit 2026: messaging and positioning framework

For the Elets marketing team. It covers the site redo, social posts and delegate emails.
Sources: `research/cto-teardown.md`, `research/was-facts.md` and `worldaisummit/speakers/speakers.json`. Anything marked **TBC** is unverified. Do not publish it until someone owns and confirms it.

## 1. Why we are copying the Inc42 CTO Summit

The CTO Summit page sells a paid ticket to a senior audience without hype. Five things do the work:

- **One outcome-framed promise:** "How India's biggest products run AI at scale".
- **An anti-positioning line** that defines the event by what it refuses.
- **An agenda that sells itself.** Each session lists specific, India-grounded "You'll learn" takeaways.
- **Scarcity on a paid pass.** Applications are reviewed, seats are capped and passes cannot be transferred.
- **FAQ answers that position the event.** They exclude the wrong audience out loud.

Our current homepage is the opposite. It is a generic theme headline with no numbers, "Five reasons the room matters" cards and a nine-row "Who Should Attend" list that includes technology vendors. We are keeping the CTO Summit's structure and its blunt tone. We are changing the category: CTO Summit is about product engineering, and ours is the one room where government, regulators and enterprise decide together how India deploys AI.

## 3. Positioning, audience, pillars

**Positioning statement.** For the officials, regulators and enterprise leaders responsible for putting AI into production in India, World AI Summit 2026 is the two-day Bengaluru summit where policy and deployment meet in one room. Other AI conferences sell the future. We examine what is already running at population scale, and the decisions and numbers behind it.

**Primary audience**
- Secretaries, Mission Directors and state IT/e-governance heads
- Regulators and quasi-regulators (SEBI, RBIH, AICTE)
- CIO, CTO, CDO and CISO in BFSI, retail and India Inc.
- Heads of AI & Data and GCC leaders

**Secondary audience**
- Founders and investors
- AI startups (50+ expected)
- Academia and think tanks
- Awards entrants

We are **not** marketing to technology vendors as delegates. They go to partnerships@.

**Pillar 1: Policy meets deployment**
- Proof: 11 confirmed government and regulator speakers, including:
  - Pankaj Kumar Pandey, IAS (Principal Secretary, e-Governance, Karnataka)
  - Dr Ravikumar Surpur, IAS (Secretary, IT & Communication, Rajasthan)
  - Aman Mittal, IAS (Joint CEO, MITRA)
  - Ram Mohan Rao (Executive Director, SEBI)
  - Sanjeev Gupta (CEO, KDEM)
- Proof: in 2025 the Karnataka IT/BT Department was host and IndiaAI Mission was co-host. Abhishek Singh (CEO, IndiaAI Mission) gave a keynote.

**Pillar 2: AI in production, not in pilots**
- Proof: 35 confirmed enterprise speakers, including:
  - Sandeep Varaganti (CEO, JioMart) and Anand Thakur (CPTO, Reliance Retail)
  - Tulshekar Gangireddy (JPMorgan Chase)
  - Deepika Sandeep (HSBC)
  - Dipayan Chakraborty (eBay)
  - Suman Guha (Croma)
  - Archana Menon (Titan)
- Proof: tracks include Enterprise AI in Production and Global Capability Centres (GCCs).
- Case-study numbers for session titles: TBC from speakers.

**Pillar 3: Built for India, at India's scale**
- Proof: the AI for Bharat and Sovereign AI & Geopolitics tracks.
- Proof: Shalini Kapoor (EkStep) and Mahesh Hariharan Iyer (RBIH) are confirmed speakers.
- Proof: second edition at the same venue. The 2025 edition drew 150+ speakers and 1,000+ stakeholders.

## 6. Gaps vs CTO Summit: what the team must supply before launch

1. **Audience % split.** We need a real delegate role breakdown by function for "Who's in the room". Until then the page shows the confirmed-speaker mix only.
2. **Named delegate contact and phone number.** CTO Summit shows "Vishvani Sharma, Delegate Team, +91…". We have no public phone number, and the page currently shows the TBC placeholder `+91 XXXXX XXXXX`.
3. **2026 partner logos and names.** None are published. Do not reuse 2025 partners as 2026 partners.
4. **Photos.** speakers.json has no `photo` values and there is no `photos/` folder. Cards use initials for now. We also need a 2025 event gallery with rights cleared.
5. **Tax line.** GST treatment is not stated. The site shows "+ taxes as applicable" as TBC. Finance must confirm the GST rate.
6. **Credits and benefits pack.** Is there a tools or credits bundle, and is the VIP dinner confirmed? Both are TBC.
7. **Sessions and takeaways.** We need 2026 session titles, times and confirmed speaker-to-session assignments. Every speaker's `session` is currently "tba".
8. **Organiser proof numbers.** We need Elets' years of publishing, events convened and monthly reach.
9. **Conflicts to fix on the live site and in coverage:**
   - **Attendance:** the Elets CIO article says 1,200+ delegates, the Global Trade Fairs listing says 1,000+, and the old homepage says "5,000+ AI leaders". Standardise on 1,200+ expected. Ask Global Trade Fairs to correct its listing, including "Entry: Free".
   - **Tracks:** the live homepage has 7 tracks (Frontier Models & Compute, and so on). The Elets CIO article of 22 Sep lists a different 7 (Generative AI & LLMs, AI Agents, and so on). The old homepage has 6. The LinkedIn "7 AI trends" post is a fourth list. Use the live homepage list everywhere and ask editorial to align the CIO article.
   - **Theme:** 2025 was "Powering India's Inclusive and Responsible AI Future" and 2026 is "Accelerating India's Journey…". Check that no 2025 theme copy remains on the 2026 pages.
   - **Pricing:** the old homepage shows a "First 300" early bird and Standard at ₹25,000/₹50,000. The current /delegate/ page shows ₹20,000/₹35,000 standard. Keep only the current prices.
   - **Headings:** the homepage has two H1s ("AI for All…" and "Voices from the Main Stage").
10. **The /delegate/ page still has 2025 early-bird copy:** "Limited to the First 150 Passes (Valid till 25th July 2025)". Remove it or replace it before any paid traffic goes to that page.
11. **Testimonials.** The current homepage quotes Harshil Mathur, Ratan Kumar Kesh and others. Before reusing any quote, confirm that it is sourced and permission is on file.

**Keywords to target**
- **Primary:** "World AI Summit", "World AI Summit 2026", "AI summit Bengaluru 2026", "AI conference India 2026".
- **Secondary:**
  - "AI summit Bangalore October 2026"
  - "enterprise AI conference India"
  - "sovereign AI India summit"
  - "GCC AI summit"
  - "World AI Awards 2026"
- **Long tail:** speaker-name searches. Each /speakers/&lt;slug&gt;/ page should target "[Name] [Org] World AI Summit", for example "Ram Mohan Rao SEBI", "Sandeep Varaganti JioMart" or "Pankaj Kumar Pandey IAS".
- **Brand disambiguation:** "World AI Summit" is also the name of an Amsterdam event. Always pair the name with "India" or "Bengaluru" in titles and on-page copy.

**CTO page mistakes we avoid**
- **Symbols in H2s.** The `</` motif sat inside the headings. Our motif lives in a `<p class="kicker">`, and H2s are clean keyword text.
- **Duplicate H2.** CTO Summit uses "Speakers" twice. We use one "Speakers" H2. A second grid, if we add one, gets a different heading.
- **Counters that crawl as "0+".** Our stats are written as static text.
- **Company names missing from speaker cards.** Our cards show name, title and organisation as text and link to the speaker page.
- **An indexable duplicate old page** (`/cto-summit-old/`). Redirect (301) or noindex any old or non-www homepage copies. The non-www cache is currently still serving the old "Save the Date / 5,000+" build.
- **No confirmed schema.** We ship Event JSON-LD on home, agenda and delegate, and FAQPage JSON-LD wherever FAQ text appears. The schema must match the visible copy exactly.
- **Two H1s.** Keep exactly one H1 per page, which fixes the current homepage.
