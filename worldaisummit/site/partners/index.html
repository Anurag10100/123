faq

Partner FAQ

Can we buy a speaking slot?

No. Partners can propose a case study, and the content team reviews it like every other session. It needs a named deployment, a customer who will stand behind it and numbers. Product demos and roadmaps are turned down.

Who actually attends?

Senior decision makers only: secretaries, mission directors and regulators, CIOs, CTOs, CDOs and CISOs, heads of AI and data, GCC leaders, founders and investors. Every delegate application is reviewed and seats are capped to keep the room senior.

Why are prices not listed?

Each package is built around what you want to prove and to whom, and category exclusivity changes the price. Write to partnerships@worldaisummit.com and you will get the deck with current rates and slot availability.

Is there an exhibition floor?

There is a startup showcase, but the summit is not built around booths. The value for partners is in hosted roundtables, curated meetings and case-study sessions in front of buyers and policymakers.

How late can we sign up?

The summit runs on 14-15 October 2026. Branding on print and stage has production deadlines, and roundtable guest lists are built weeks ahead, so the earlier you write, the more of the package you get.
