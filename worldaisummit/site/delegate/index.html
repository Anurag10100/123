pass_faq

Pass questions

Can I get a refund if I cannot attend?

The refund and cancellation policy will be shared with your confirmation. Write to registration@worldaisummit.com before paying if you need it in advance.

Can I transfer my pass to a colleague?

No. Passes are issued to the approved applicant and cannot be shared or transferred. If you can no longer attend, write to registration@worldaisummit.com and a colleague can apply in their own name for review.

Will I get a GST invoice in my company's name?

Yes. Tick the GST invoice option on the application form and our team will collect your company's billing name, address and GSTIN before payment. Pass prices are plus taxes as applicable.

I am a government official. How do nominations work?

Departments and missions can nominate officers by writing to secretariat@worldaisummit.com with the name, designation and department of each nominee. Our team will confirm the pass category for each nomination.

Is there a discount for teams?

Yes. Book three or more delegates from the same organisation and get 10% off. Each delegate still submits an application. Write to registration@worldaisummit.com to set up a group booking.

What is included in the pass?

Both days of the summit on 14-15 October 2026, all seven tracks, the World AI Awards evening and meals on both days. The VIP Delegate pass adds priority seating, the speaker lounge, the networking dinner, special sessions on GenAI, Agentic AI and AI Safety, and an invite to the Government and Investor Roundtable.

The people deciding how India deploys AI will be in Bengaluru on 14 October. The question is whether you will.
